How ratings work

Ratings use Elo — the same system chess uses, starting everyone at 1000. Before each match, both sides' ratings predict who should win — beat a stronger opponent and you gain more, lose to a weaker one and you lose more. Every point gained is lost by someone else, so the rating pool never inflates.

This month restarts everyone at 1000 on the 1st, so a rough month never follows you around — above 1000 means you've gained ground since the reset. Last 6 months never resets but weights recent form: results under 3 months old count fully, older ones fade, and past 6 months they stop counting — so the number reflects current form, and numbers shift slightly as time passes even with no new games.

Both need + matches across 2+ sessions in their window (that month, or the last 6 months) before they're ranked — fewer than that and you're marked provisional, still shown, just not ranked yet, so one big session alone can't top the table.
